VELOURA — WESTERN WEAR SHOP DEMO
================================
Open index.html in a browser to preview the storefront.

Included:
- Responsive homepage and collection/category sections
- Western dress product grid with category filters and search
- Shopping bag with add/remove and quantity controls (saved in localStorage)
- Demo checkout that creates a local demo order ID
- Demo order tracking (try VL-2026-1042)
- Newsletter interaction and mobile navigation
- Provided logo artwork in assets/logo.jpg

Notes:
- This is a front-end prototype. Payments, real customer accounts, inventory,
  order fulfilment, live courier tracking and email subscription require a backend
  and integrations before launching as a live shop.
- Product photography is loaded from Unsplash and needs an internet connection.
- Prices and product details are sample/demo data; replace them with real inventory.
